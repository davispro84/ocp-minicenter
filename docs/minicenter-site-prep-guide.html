OCP Mini-Center — Site Preparation Guide

DRAFT v0.1 — 2026-09-29 — Primary Design tier (520kW, two-skid). Customer/contractor-facing: what the site must provide before the trucks arrive.

The "drop-and-run" premise only holds if the site is correctly stubbed up first. This is the civil/electrical/mechanical package handed to the customer's local contractor, typically 6-8 weeks ahead of delivery.

1. Civil & Spatial — The Pads

Two distinct leveled foundations, not one monolithic slab, separated by a dedicated umbilical corridor.

ItemRequirement
Skid A (Compute) foundation40ft High-Cube containers are designed to be supported at their four ISO corner castings, not mid-span. Either a level 45'×12' concrete pad, or four deep concrete piers (sonotubes) drilled below the local frost line, sized to carry ~14,100kg without settling.
Skid B (Power/Mechanical) foundationStandard 40'×10' concrete equipment pad.
Pad separation / umbilical corridorMinimum 8-10ft gap between pads — clearance for the adiabatic cooler's air intake, the generator's exhaust, and technician access. An above-ground cable tray or shallow trench spans this gap carrying the 3" Victaulic fluid lines, the paralleled Cam-Lok power cables, and the Amphenol/MIL-spec multi-pin PLC handshake connector (controls interlock between Skid A and Skid B — see Basis of Design Section 12).

2. Electrical & Fuel — The Power Stub

Deployment modelCustomer must provide
Stranded gas (SG600 generator) Regulated natural gas pipeline stub-up to the Skid B pad. A 600kW spark-ignited engine draws roughly 6,400 SCFH (independently checked against genset efficiency — matches the vendor-adjacent 6,000-7,500 CFH range well). Requires a dedicated pressure-reducing valve (PRV), moisture scrubber/knockout pot (flared gas runs "wet"), and a manual shutoff.
Grid-tied (Rust Belt / decommissioned industrial / telecom) Utility-provided pad-mount transformer adjacent to Skid A, stepping down to 480Y/277V 3-phase, terminating at a fused disconnect stubbed to Skid A's ATS input. Diligence item, not assumed: confirm the interconnection agreement's capacity is actually still live, not decommissioned along with whatever plant used to be there.
Grounding (both models) Continuous perimeter ground ring (bare copper) trenched around both pads, bonded to driven ground rods, with pigtails stubbed up for the container chassis, generator frame, and switchgear ground bus.

3. Mechanical — The Adiabatic Water Feed

Corrected 2026-09-29: an earlier draft of this section carried the water consumption figure from the original 260kW/2-rack design (100-150 gal/hr) forward without rescaling for the 520kW/4-rack upgrade. Recomputed using the same first-principles method from the original hot-climate feasibility check: at 550kW (Skid B's actual target), the 100%-evaporative worst-case upper bound is ~231 gal/hr, roughly double the stale figure. Real consumption will be lower than this bound (real units split load between the dry coil and the wetted pre-cool stage), but the *supply line and any storage tank* should be sized against the corrected worst case, not the old number.
ItemRequirement
Water supply lineDedicated 3/4"-1" line, 40-60 PSI.
Volume, peak adiabatic operationSustain up to ~230 gal/hr (corrected worst-case upper bound at 550kW — see callout above). If no municipal water is available, customer installs a local storage tank + booster pump sized against this figure, not the smaller stale one.
Freeze protectionSupply line buried below the regional frost line, stubbed up inside a heated/insulated riser, terminating in a backflow preventer and mechanical float valve at the cooler.

The Customer Responsibility Matrix

Site contractor handles: excavation, both concrete pads, the ground ring, and all utility/fuel/water pipe stub-ups. The vendor delivers: two skids, on-site Cam-Lok + Victaulic + Amphenol mating across the umbilical corridor, and commissioning.
