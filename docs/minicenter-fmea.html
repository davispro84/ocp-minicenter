OCP Mini-Center — Failure Modes & Effects Analysis

DRAFT v0.1 — companion to the Basis of Design; Severity/Likelihood/Detection are engineering-judgment estimates (1–5 scale), NOT derived from field failure-rate data — treat RPN as a relative prioritization tool, not a statistical guarantee

Systematic failure analysis across five subsystems, built to actually earn "ironclad" rather than assert it. Corrects two specific errors that showed up (and repeated) in outside AI-assisted research, credits the ideas that were right, and adds several failure modes that hadn't been raised yet — particularly around remote monitoring, which is the part that makes "zero on-site maintenance" real instead of aspirational.

ARCHITECTURE PIVOT (2026-09-28): the Mini-Center dropped the Diablo 400 800VDC bus and the Chilldyne negative-pressure CDU — commercial OEM AI racks (GB200 NVL72-class) take standard AC and convert to DC internally, so a proprietary DC-bus facility can't host real customer hardware. New baseline: standard 480V/415V AC straight to the rack, standard OCP BMQC-compliant positive-pressure CDU (Motivair MCDU-25), leak risk mitigated via welded stainless containment pans + zoned leak-detection rope rather than a vacuum loop. Rows below updated accordingly; see `lib/engineering_math.py`'s Mini-Center section for the full rationale. The original 4-rack Pod elsewhere in this repo keeps the Diablo 400 architecture — it's a deliberate hyperscale-showcase design, not superseded.
TWO-SKID ARCHITECTURE (2026-09-29): the Primary Design tier scaled to 4 racks/520kW, which doesn't physically fit generator + heat rejection + compute in one 40ft box (a real, checked volumetric constraint, not a judgment call). Split into Skid A (Compute) — 40ft HC container, 4 racks, 2 CDUs, 1200A switchgear/ATS, 10kVA Aux UPS, IMP partition wall — and Skid B (Mechanical/Power) — standard flatbed, 600kW-class generator + 550kW adiabatic cooler. Mated on-site via Cam-Lok power cables (paralleled per phase for the 1200A total), 3" grooved Victaulic fluid couplings (matches Motivair's real 3" connection size), and a ruggedized Amphenol/MIL-spec multi-pin connector for the inter-skid PLC handshake — deliberately mirroring already-proven industry (Crusoe-style) multi-skid deployment practice, not an invented scheme. Rows below re-scoped by skid where the risk profile genuinely differs (structural/transit, leak containment) — most electrical/cooling/monitoring findings apply to Skid A regardless of the split.
NEW OPEN QUESTIONS FROM THE SPLIT (2026-09-29), not yet resolved: (1) exact control logic for the leak-trip sequence — when Skid A's zoned rope detects fluid, what precisely happens to the CDU pumps and the main ATS, in what order; (2) confirming the out-of-band monitoring stack's independent battery (already decided, FMEA 5.2) actually covers the Starlink dish specifically through a full blackout, not just the edge gateway; (3) skid-to-skid comms protocol between Skid B's generator and Skid A's switchgear/CDUs — Modbus TCP over ruggedized ethernet is the working assumption (reuses a protocol Motivair's CDU already speaks), not yet confirmed as the final choice.
Two corrections carried through this whole document:
1) BBU ride-through is NOT "3-to-5 minutes" — the real OCP Diablo 400 spec (Sec 9.2.1.1) states 45–90 seconds at 100% load. Every mitigation involving battery ride-through below is sized against the real number.
2) "NEMA 4X / IP55" is not a valid equivalence — NEMA 4X corresponds to IP66. Every enclosure-rating item below specifies IP66, and HEPA filtration is replaced with MERV 13–16 (adequate for dust/pollen exclusion at a fraction of the pressure-drop and replacement burden — HEPA actively works against the zero-maintenance goal).

5. Controls, Monitoring & Zero-Maintenance Enablement

This category didn't exist in either outside research pass, and it's the one that actually determines whether "zero on-site maintenance" is real or aspirational — a perfectly reliable machine that fails silently with nobody watching isn't zero-maintenance, it's zero-warning.
