OCP Mini-Center — Project Overview

A truck-deployable, 520kW modular AI compute facility for stranded power sites — the single document that ties design basis, architecture, target customer, and current project status together in one place.

OVERVIEW — DRAFT v0.1 — 2026-09-30. Rolls up the companion documents below; not a replacement for any of them.

1. What This Project Is

A two-skid, crane-set, cable-terminated data hall module built to deploy in days at sites that already have power but no facility to use it — flare-gas pads, curtailed renewable interconnects, decommissioned industrial substations. It hosts standard commercial OEM AI hardware (GB200 NVL72-class racks) on standard 480V/415V 3-phase AC and OCP BMQC-compliant positive-pressure liquid cooling — no proprietary DC bus to qualify hardware against.

This is the Primary Design tier (520kW / 4-rack, two-skid). A lower-density Standard Tier (10-30kW/rack, rural telecom/SCADA/rugged compute, single-box, air- or DX-cooled) shares the same container shell, electrical room, climate kits, and shock-isolated sub-floor, differing only in the compute-room payload. A separate, still-valid project — the original 4-rack OCP Pod (Diablo 400 / 800VDC bus, hyperscale reference architecture) — lives in a different repo and was never superseded by this one; the two just diverged into different design spaces.

2. Architecture — The Two Skids

Splitting into two skids was forced by a real volumetric constraint, not a preference: a 600kW-class generator plus a 4.5m compute row leaves only 1.7m for switchgear inside a single 40ft box. The split deliberately mirrors already-proven industry practice (Crusoe-style stranded-power deployment).

3. Who This Is For

Two specific buyer profiles — not general enterprise IT or colocation — positioned against incumbents (Vertiv SmartMod, Schneider EcoStruxure) whose modular DC products assume an existing, prepared campus (central chilled-water plant, large utility drop, monolithic slab) and typically top out around 10-20kW/rack air-cooled.

ProfileWhoThe needThe pitch
A — Neo-Cloud AI OperatorCrusoe/CoreWeave-adjacentCapital + compute demand, bottlenecked by multi-year utility interconnection queues520kW of OEM-compatible liquid-cooled compute, deployable in days once the site is stubbed up — no interconnection queue, no chiller plant, survives unmanned in West Texas
B — Energy Asset OwnerOil & gas majors, renewable developersMegawatts of stranded power (flared gas, curtailed wind/solar) generating zero revenue, no in-house AI infra expertiseTurn waste power into compute revenue via a turnkey, zero-maintenance appliance — pour a pad, pipe in the gas, we handle the FMEA and extreme-weather engineering

Real target geography: Permian Basin / West Texas (flared gas, hot), Bakken / North Dakota (flared gas, cold), decommissioned Rust Belt industrial sites (existing grid interconnection, moderate climate) — chosen because stranded-power infrastructure and planet-extreme remoteness are anti-correlated, not because the design can't handle more extreme sites.
